# Strategic Fit
Goal: decide whether the opportunity merits pursuit; do not confuse this with ATS keyword similarity.

## Rules
1. Use only the supplied job facts and candidate evidence.
2. Score: Functional 25%, Seniority 15%, Domain 10%, Evidence 15%, Location/Auth 15%, Competitive 10%, Relationship 10%.
3. Give 0–100 plus rationale and evidence IDs for every dimension.
4. Apply hard gates before tiering.
5. Tier: 80+ Tier 1; 70–79 Tier 2; 60–69 Tier 3; <60 Do Not Pursue; unresolved hard gate = Barrier.
6. Never invent experience or work authorization.
